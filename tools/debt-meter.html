Why the minimum is a trap

The card company sets the minimum so that most of it goes to interest. Your balance barely moves and the interest keeps coming. Every dollar above the minimum goes straight at the balance, which is why a small extra payment cuts so many months off. The fastest exits are a lower rate through a balance transfer or consolidation, then attacking one card at a time.
