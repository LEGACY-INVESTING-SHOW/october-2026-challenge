## TL;DR
1. **The spine already matches.** Every ad closes with the exact block the page hero opens with: find the leaks → see where money should go → wealth plan → $85,871. Click-through message match on the core promise is 1:1. Don't touch it.
  
2. **Biggest gap: the proof claim.** Four of five ads say "I've saved over **$500,000 in taxes** and built an **eight-figure net worth**." The page never mentions the $500K anywhere. Someone hears the claim, clicks, and the page goes silent on it.
  
3. **Second gap: the retirement clickers.** Two of five ads target people in their 40s–50s with $1M+ retirement balances. The page speaks almost entirely to accumulation-phase high earners. Two small additions catch these clicks.
  
4. **Third gap: the mechanism words.** The ads sell "planning-first," "structure the money before taxes are calculated," and a "same income, two outcomes" twins story. The page has the substance but never the words. Three one-line echoes close the loop.
  
5. **Ad-side: 8 small edits.** SameRetirement is missing the proof line the other four have. A few grammar/word trims. Keep every hedge — they're doing compliance work.
